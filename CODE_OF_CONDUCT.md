# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

ForgeCI is committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and constructive community for everyone.

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and professional.
- Discuss ideas rather than people.
- Accept constructive feedback.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, and personal attacks.
- Focus on building a reliable and secure project.

## Enforcement

Unacceptable behavior may be addressed by the project maintainers. Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or take other reasonable actions to keep project spaces constructive and safe.
